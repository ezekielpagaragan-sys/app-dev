# Toy Story

## A. Introduction

**Toy Story** is a groundbreaking animated film about toys that come to life whenever humans aren't around. It follows the rivalry and eventual friendship between a loyal cowboy doll and a flashy space ranger action figure. The movie is a classic because it mixes humor, adventure, and heartfelt emotion in a way that both kids and adults enjoy.

- **Genre:** Animation, Adventure, Comedy, Family
- **Release Year:** 1995
- **Studio:** Pixar Animation Studios
- **Director:** John Lasseter

---

## B. Synopsis

The story begins in a boy named Andy's bedroom, where his toys secretly come alive when he leaves the room. Woody, a pull-string cowboy, is Andy's favorite toy and the unofficial leader of the group. Everything changes on Andy's birthday when he receives Buzz Lightyear, a high-tech space ranger who believes he is a real hero. Woody becomes jealous as Buzz steals the spotlight, and a fight between them leaves both toys lost outside the house. They end up in the hands of Sid, a cruel neighbor who destroys and modifies toys for fun. Woody and Buzz must put aside their differences, work together, and race to get back to Andy before his family moves away.

### Story Highlights

1. Buzz arrives and disrupts the toys' routine
2. Woody and Buzz get separated from Andy
3. They escape Sid's house with help from the mutant toys
4. The two heroes chase down the moving truck

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie
I chose *Toy Story* because it brings back great childhood memories and still feels fresh today. It shows how a simple idea, toys coming to life, can become a story about friendship and growing up.

### Things I Liked
- **Characters:** Woody and Buzz have a great dynamic that goes from rivals to best friends.
- **Humor:** The jokes work for both kids and adults.
- **Animation:** As the first fully computer-animated feature film, it looks impressive for its time.

### Personal Rating
**9/10**

### Recommendation
> I highly recommend *Toy Story* to anyone who wants a fun, emotional, and well-written movie. It is great for families, but **anyone** who enjoys a good story will like it.
